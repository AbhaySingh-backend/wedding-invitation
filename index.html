Getting here

  • By air

    Chaudhary Charan Singh airport is about 40 minutes from Kaiserbagh. Send us your flight and someone will be there.

    Charbagh station is 15 minutes from the venue. Tell us your train and a car will be waiting.

  • Where to stay

    Rooms are kept for guests from out of town, near Hazratganj. Mention it in your reply.

    Evenings are pleasant and nights drop to about 15°C. Bring a light shawl for the Walima.
